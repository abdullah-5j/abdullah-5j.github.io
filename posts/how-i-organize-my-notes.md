It is easy to finish a lab and move on. It is harder — and more valuable — to remember why something worked.

## Keep notes while solving

Instead of saving just the final commands, write down the questions you asked and how you answered them.

## Separate observations from conclusions

An observation might be *"a web service responds on port 8080."* A conclusion would explain how that affects your approach.

## Finish with a takeaway

The best note is one you can revisit months later and still understand.

**A useful format:** observation → hypothesis → test → result → lesson.
