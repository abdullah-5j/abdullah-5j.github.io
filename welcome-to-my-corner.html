Welcome to my corner of the internet

◷ 2026-10-09▤ Thoughts◷ 2 min read

Hey there! Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

This space is where I'll share cybersecurity writeups, CTF notes, experiments, and thoughts as I learn. Sometimes that means a full technical walkthrough. Other times, it's a short idea or a lesson learned while trying to solve a problem.

What to expect

  • Writeups: Hack The Box, TryHackMe, CTF challenges, and other labs.
  • Research notes: Tools, techniques, concepts, and the reasoning behind them.
  • Thoughts: Reflections on learning, mistakes, projects, and progress.

I want the notes here to be useful to my future self as much as to anyone else who finds them. Clear explanations, reproducible steps, and lessons that stick.

Learn something. Document it. Share what matters.

See you in the next post. — Verox
